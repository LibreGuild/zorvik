Welcome! This Bootcamp takes you from **"what is a network?"** to testing, mocking and load testing real APIs like a pro. No experience needed.

## How a lesson works

Every lesson has the same rhythm:

1. **Read.** A few minutes of plain words and pictures. No jargon without an explanation.
2. **Lab.** You press **Start lab**. Zorvik starts small practice servers on *your own computer* and switches to the normal workbench, with a **Lab Guide** on the right. You do each step for real; the guide ticks it off the moment you get it right.
3. **Quick check.** Three short questions to lock it in.
4. **Reward.** XP, levels, badges and a streak for learning on consecutive days.

```flow
Read -> Lab -> Quick check -> Reward
```

> [!tip] You learn in the real app
> The workbench you use in labs is the same one you use at work. Nothing is simulated: every request really goes over the network, just to a server on your own machine.

## The Training Bootcamp workspace

Labs happen in a special workspace called **Training Bootcamp**. It is always there, pinned at the top of the workspace menu, and it can't be deleted, so you can't break anything that matters. If it gets messy, **Reset** it from the Academy menu: your progress and badges stay.

## Switching between Academy and Workbench

At the top of the window there is a switch:

- **Academy** is this place: the course map, lessons and your rewards.
- **Workbench** is the normal app. During a lab, the Lab Guide stays docked on the right.

## XP, levels and badges

| You… | You earn |
|---|---|
| finish a lab step | 10 XP |
| finish a lesson | 50 XP |
| answer a quiz question right the first time | 5 XP |
| finish a unit | 100 XP and its badge |

Levels come with ranks, from **Newbie Node** to **Wire Wizard**. Some badges are secret-ish: try finishing a lab without hints.

Units follow a recommended order, but nothing is locked. Already know DNS? Jump ahead, or take the unit's **test-out** quiz to earn its badge quickly.
